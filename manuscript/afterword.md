# Afterword — What Would Make This a Theory?

Before the South Devon Railway bought another leather valve, Isambard Kingdom Brunel wanted a guarantee. Four years earlier he had urged the directors to adopt atmospheric propulsion. By August 19, 1848, their trains had carried passengers from Exeter to Newton, and the pumps had consumed far more coal than he expected. He reckoned he could cut fuel use by a third if nearly the whole valve were replaced. Would the patentees undertake to keep it tight? [Brunel’s two reports, chapter VI](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/41210/old/41210-h/41210-h.htm)

The directors made the machinery available to anyone who wanted to continue the work at their own expense. For passenger service they chose locomotives. An explanation of how air pressure came to move the first train still has to account for why the last ran so soon afterward. [The railway decision](05-how-disequilibrium-builds-a-new-pathway.md#chapter-notes)

At Fowey Consols fourteen years earlier, two sealed counters had agreed on every stroke an engine made during a public trial. Near the bottom of the shaft a broken bucket had left one lift raising no water for almost three hours. A skeptic could accept both readings and still dispute the result. [The witnesses’ account](05-how-disequilibrium-builds-a-new-pathway.md#chapter-notes)

The claim that an assembly can turn an energy difference to a new use is weaker than the claim that such assemblies will spread. The first train running a mile could settle whether air pressure would move a carriage. The prospect of a second line turned on what its fares could pay to replace. To test the stronger claim we have to identify, before seeing the outcome, a route that could have worked, who could have met its first costs, and what share of the return was available for a successor.

## What changes the next starting point?

Before Tang Tingshu had his railway, he had a price for a ton of coal at the Kaiping pit and a second price at the point where the carters would leave it for shipment. The cart ride nearly doubled the cost. His proposed output would take three hundred carts a day. These figures survive from the period when he still had to persuade someone to pay for the railway. [Tang’s survey](05-how-disequilibrium-builds-a-new-pathway.md#three-hundred-carts)

Use Tang’s prices to estimate what a better pump would have earned with only the carts to take coal away. Then estimate what a railway would have earned with the old pits supplying it. If either alone would have paid handsomely, the claim that owners had to solve both problems together loses force. Accounts from owners who kept the carts could help settle the comparison.

This is one use I see for dialectics. The coal provided fuel for the pump on which access to further coal depended. The old winding equipment kept its use when a steam pump let miners go deeper. And extra hours of pumping took on a quite different value when the water went below a workable seam. Those are three different claims about the same pit. We can look for the return of coal to the boiler, for the work horses continued to do and for the depth at which crews could enter. A theory that traces those changes to indeterminacy in nature has another obligation. Familiar deterministic models can also produce thresholds and mutual dependencies. What further observation favors the proposed origin? [The working arrangements at Saltom](01-the-square-with-one-answer.md) · [The three dialectical principles](../research/sources.md#s130)

At a mine the date on which a crew could first enter a seam is a reasonable measure of when a capacity arose. In biology even dating the event can be hard. A grain of Australian zircon carries evidence of an early wet surface. A much younger hot-spring deposit preserves traces of organisms already living there. To say that life arose quickly *once conditions were ready*, we need to identify those conditions without first using the presence of life as proof that they were ready. We need to allow for the loss of early rocks, and for the fact that anyone asking the question will find themselves on a planet where life began in time for observers to evolve. [The zircon, the hot springs and the timing problem](03-life-makes-its-conditions.md#how-long-did-the-first-step-take)

At Lost City organisms draw on chemical differences between vent fluid and seawater. Eric Smith and Harold Morowitz propose that metabolism gave the early Earth access to energy otherwise slow to dissipate. How fast would the same fluid react with the available minerals? How fast once an organization could put some of the gain into renewing its components? Include the cost of forming it. A network that would process a great deal of fluid once established may take longer to form than a vent lasts. [Smith and Morowitz](../research/sources.md#s140) · [The chemistry at Lost City](03-life-makes-its-conditions.md#the-chemistry-a-vent-makes-possible)

## A model that can fail

The Fowey witnesses counted work at the pumps. Apply that boundary to a coal mine and it leaves out the fuel the engine makes accessible. Apply it to a metal mine and it leaves out the ore that paid for coal. Before comparing assemblies, specify what crosses the boundary, for how long, and what counts as a successor. Run Carquillat’s cards again and there are two pictures, still one loom. A cell that divides leaves two organisms capable of dividing. [Cornish fuel and ore](05-how-disequilibrium-builds-a-new-pathway.md) · [Assembly and occurrence](../research/sources.md#s02) · [Biological self-production](../research/sources.md#s33)

A test of the proposed energetic preference should include a case in which drawing the most energy and producing the most successors point to different winners. Give one arrangement a larger flow of usable energy and a costly part that only an outside supplier can replace. Give another a smaller flow and a way to replace its parts out of its own production. Record the flow, the cost, the stock of spares and what counts as a new instance before the comparison begins. Once the first supplier is gone and the spares run out, the larger flow alone should cease to predict which arrangement spreads. Restore the supply or let the first arrangement use some of its gain to make the missing part, and its prospects should change. The model by itself can only give back the dependencies we put into it. The test is to measure the return and the supply of parts independently, predict which arrangement will spread, and count new installations. If expansion keeps following outside finance regardless of how much of the gain reaches the builder, outside finance has a better claim to explain that history.

Pommet’s forty francs show why the recipient matters. A sale might bring money into the workshop without giving the weaver enough to live on while she made the next piece. Evidence for that return would have to follow the wages home. The court record stops at the ruling. [The Lyon hearing](09-the-system-that-produces-its-tomorrow.md#chapter-notes)

Profit is a serious rival in a mine. Buyers might pay so much for a modest output that an owner expands a pit which releases less net energy than its neighbor. The theory needs to say beforehand whether it predicts more working mines, more fuel available across a district, or more entropy produced in it, then accept the answer for the outcome it chose. For discovery, shared equations might already predict precisely where physicists converge. An energetic account earns an additional claim if it can explain which measurements people could afford to make and preserve, and predict a difference where the mathematical opportunities were alike. For a maximum rate of entropy production, specify the feasible alternatives and the interval in advance. A higher rate for one shift can exhaust the fuel a rival would still be using next year. [Joule, the mine and the rate comparison](02-the-gradient-and-the-barrier.md#where-the-heat-goes) · [Poincaré’s and Einstein’s different results](04-the-discovery-that-became-possible.md)

With Muennighoff’s model, keep the spelling questions fixed and vary the time allowed, the prompt and access to an outside check. Count correct answers lost on a second try as well as mistakes repaired. For a self-model, ask whether a record of the system’s own attention lets it do better with the same time and equipment. For autopoiesis, follow the gain further. Which components does better control help replace, and what makes a replacement controller? [The additional attempt](11-why-the-world-builds-artificial-intelligence.md#an-extra-consonant) · [Modeling attention](../research/sources.md#s94)

Test the predictions about artificial cognition and data centers separately. Specify a capability, the resources it requires and when it should appear. If it keeps arising on dispersed equipment, that weakens the prediction about where machines must be gathered even if the broader prediction about artificial cognition holds.

## Where the laws come from

With the cosmological constant set to zero, Adam Riess’s calculation from distant supernovae gave a negative density of matter. A proposed history of the constants has the opposite difficulty. Its author already knows the value the universe produced. What second quantity can the same history predict before anyone measures it? [Riess’s notebook and the proposed relation to the weak scale](book/13-fine-tuning-paradox.md)

Start with a ratio whose value is independent of human choices about units. Let a model produce it through the interactions of its parts. Which rules have to be present from the outset? Can very different starting conditions give the same measured ratio? Does the assembly draw work from some identifiable difference in order to restore the ratio after a disturbance? A barrier between two states can also keep a system stable for a very long time. Change the energy supply and the barrier separately. If an ordinary barrier explains the recovery just as well, self-production has yet to earn its place in the account. If the model requires its starting values to be set with just the precision it was supposed to explain, the fine-tuning problem has merely moved. [Decoherence and metastability](06-from-uncertainty-to-an-assembled-universe.md#how-long-a-vacuum-state-can-last)

I think reality is indeterminate at its foundations and that the universe and some of its laws probably formed. Absolute nothingness, on that view, would require a certainty that reality does not admit. That gives a reason to investigate formation. It gives us neither a value for a constant nor an ordering for a series of events if time itself belongs to what forms. The imbalance between matter and antimatter is a number to account for. The difference between left and right in weak interactions calls for a different observation. Producing one regularity would put a physical version of the theory under a valuable obligation. The same assumptions could then be tried on something its author had not already used to choose them. [The case for assembled laws](06-from-uncertainty-to-an-assembled-universe.md)

At Fowey the witnesses deducted three hours of work from the lift whose bucket broke. Another engineer could read the report and allow two hours and forty-three minutes instead. I would want a reader to have the same opportunity here, to see where the energy went, which new capacity it bought, and where the evidence permits a different reckoning. [The signed trial record](05-how-disequilibrium-builds-a-new-pathway.md#chapter-notes)
