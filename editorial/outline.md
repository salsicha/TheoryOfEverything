# The World That Builds Itself

## A Material History of Everything

**Current chapter outline:** twelve chapters in three parts, with a prologue, epilogue, and methodological afterword. Chapters 1–3 establish the framework; chapters 4–6 work backward from familiar discoveries through steam engines to the possible assembly of the universe; chapters 7–12 follow human society through collective intelligence, economic self-production, cybernetics, AI as assembly, and productive deployment. All twelve chapters have prose drafts. This outline and the chapter files use the same reading order.

## Central argument

The central argument is a creation cycle grounded in ontological uncertainty. An exploitable energy gradient and constraints on accessible configurations jointly make an opening. Assembly is the next organizational level built from earlier capabilities, with a new relationship that enables energy extraction. Continuation concerns the frequency with which that organization occurs and is renewed; the theory requires autopoiesis in the system producing that recurrence. Further creation is dialectical: interpenetration of opposites, negation of the negation, and quantity into quality describe how the organization transforms its own conditions and makes a successor possible.

The cycle has a proposed energetic direction. The next assembly level must enable an energy-access capability that its components lacked in their previous organization. Whether that capability yields a sustained net gain, renews its productive conditions, or becomes more frequent is then an empirical question. The hypothesis predicts that returns reaching regenerative operations favor continued occurrence and further assembly. Identify the construction relationship and proposed access mechanism before measuring outcomes; a larger energy bill or an imposed definition cannot establish the predicted advantage.

This is a hypothesis about effective access, recurring organization, and its autopoietic cause. Specify the assembly, producing system, interval, and occurrence measure; distinguish copy number, production, loss, and renewal rates. Record construction, operation, and maintenance costs. Stable remnants can preserve inputs for later processes, but stability alone does not establish continuation in the clarified cycle. The stronger maximum-entropy-production conjecture requires further physical conditions and comparisons.

**Ontological foundation:** reality is ontologically uncertain in the book's account. Chapter 6 grounds fine-tuning and cosmological assembly in that premise: physical realization and formal relations become definite together, and established relations constrain further formation. Distinguish constitutive indeterminacy from incomplete knowledge, statistical uncertainty, and the structured quantum vacuum. The foundation motivates the physical explanation; a model must still derive observable consequences.

**Extended hypothesis:** the emergence of the universe and the emergence of AI are proposed as natural processes of assembly. AI and its physical supports develop through material resources, formal organization, learning, and maintained institutions. The universe and fundamental constants are probably assembled in the book's working conjecture. These claims require mechanisms and evidence; they do not require a unique outcome across all possible histories.

**Differentiation, symmetry, and disequilibrium:** within chapter 6, the proposed emergence of definite relations leads to the question of differentiation. Symmetry breaking supplies physical mechanisms once states and interactions are specified. The chapter then distinguishes matter predominance, interaction handedness, dimensions, time, and usable gradients before extending the account to laws and constants.

**Fine-tuning extension:** from this ontological foundation, progressive constraints, stationary principles, symmetry breaking, recursive maintenance, and cumulative assembly could explain apparently coordinated physical conditions as products of development. Chapter 6 joins the foundation and the proposed mechanism in one argument. Derive the parameter relations and their dependence on prior states; do not assume a distribution over independently selected settings.

Constraints do more than exclude alternatives. They can concentrate search, reduce the cost of a transition, or direct enough existing energy and organized effort toward a barrier to make a costly first assembly viable. A successful assembly can then increase the resources and knowledge available for subsequent transitions.

**Dialectical interpretation (I71):** all three principles are required in the proposed account of further creation. Identify mutually constitutive opposites, the preserved and reorganized capabilities, and the quantitative change that enables a new qualitative regime. Ontological uncertainty supplies the proposed openness of categorical boundaries. The application belongs to this book; it does not attribute the terminology to Einstein or make observed thresholds proof of the ontology. [S130](../research/sources.md#s130)

**Explanatory order:** ontological uncertainty → exploitable gradient and constrained search space → next organizational level enabling energy extraction → recurring occurrence sustained through autopoiesis → dialectical further creation. Interpenetration of opposites, negation of the negation, and quantity into quality govern that last transition. Material and formal relations jointly shape the process; cybernetics regulates its productive return.

Cybernetics supplies the account of regulation within that sequence. Hierarchical and recurrent processes can coordinate activity; self-modeling can make the system's own capacities and limits available for further regulation. Trained artificial networks extend the question of how useful internal organization is assembled. Their history also separates the success of a capability from the methodological expectations of the researchers who named its field.

For social systems, the book synthesizes Marx and Luhmann through cybernetics. Material production sustains the participants and communications that organize production; communications establish distinctions, obligations, and rules that direct subsequent material activity. Feedback connects consequences to the preservation or revision of those relations. Chapters 9 and 10 develop this jointly material and Platonic system through the reciprocal dependence of production and regulation. The synthesis belongs to the book and preserves both thinkers' distinct explanatory commitments.

**Scope:** the creation cycle and its energetic preference are the central hypothesis. The maximum-entropy-production conjecture is a stronger claim requiring additional physical conditions and comparisons. Chapter 2 defines extraction, costs, boundaries, levels, and rates; chapter 3 distinguishes autopoiesis from reproduction; chapter 6 develops the ontological premise. Historical evidence, physical mechanisms, and the synthesis have distinct roles.

**Standard of demonstration:** use natural experiments and explicit causal inference to demonstrate empirical causal arguments. Historical and observational evidence can identify effects without a newly conducted experiment when the comparison and its assumptions are defensible. Each claim must specify the proposed cause, outcome, population or system, time interval, counterfactual comparison, assignment process, competing explanations, and uncertainty. Distinguish an illustration, an association, an identified effect, and a broader causal hypothesis. A historical event is not automatically a natural experiment; mathematical deductions and cosmological origin models require evidence appropriate to their claims. The [afterword](../manuscript/afterword.md) develops this standard. The adoption of a method is not a claim that the manuscript's proposed studies have been completed.

12. **The Machines That Build the Next Machines.** Follow a computational design into fabrication, a trial and ordinary service. Bring back the Cornish duty report to test a performance claim, and the workshop to ask who can put a gain back into production.

**Epilogue — The World That Builds Its Next Question.** Return from the connected data center to a challenged performance claim, the face in silk and the first load raised from the shaft. Who decides what the next shift can build?

**Afterword — What Would Make This a Theory?** Test gradient and constraint jointly, the next level's access capability, autopoietic recurrence, and dialectical further creation. Predeclare hierarchy, boundaries, and occurrence measures. Separate the definition of an energy-enabling assembly from predictions about net gain and sustained production. Include counterexamples to the necessity claim and tests of all three dialectical relations.

### 9. The System That Produces Its Tomorrow

### 11. Why the World Builds Artificial Intelligence
