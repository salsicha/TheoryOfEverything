## Mathematical support in the prose

Chapter 1 distinguishes a philosophical premise about existence from a vacuum measurement. Chapter 2 separates an asymmetry in the weak force, the net amount of ordinary matter and the thermodynamic direction of time. Chapter 3 measures the heat two different flows can carry through the same vessel. Chapter 5 follows the carbon balance of a cycle at a vent. Chapter 7 compares seven frameworks for predicting which structures persist and which new assembly can follow. The folding chapter and the joint chapter on Earth and Fermi separate the number of imaginable states from the time required for a viable transition. ## Writing conventions

Name the chapter’s central concept and the relationship it explains. A reader looking for autopoiesis or dialectical materialism should be able to find it in the contents. Let the claim determine which scenes belong in the chapter. State what the reader is being asked to believe while the evidence is still in view. Give an inference in terms of what happened or what the explanation predicts. Explain why the recorded choice or outcome favors that account, what a plausible alternative would predict, and which part of the argument the record leaves open. A second story earns its place by resolving an uncertainty in the first, exposing a limit or carrying an established result further.

Open at the first decision or observation that changes what someone can do. Give the reader the place, the person and the immediate problem. At Kaiping, Tang Tingshu goes ashore at Lutai. The road between the coalfield and his steamer will decide how much extra coal he can sell. Leave the scene once the decision is clear. Give the reader the reasoning needed to see what the decision establishes.

Build scenes from records, objects and observations a reader can check. Identify a remembered conversation as a recollection and a habitual practice as such. Quote documented words when the wording matters. Let a request, an evasion or a silence carry its own meaning. Give background in the action or the narration. A dream belongs in an opening only when the record shows that it changed what someone did on waking.

Give the action to the person or object that performs it. Name what the record lets the reader see, hear or measure. Keep an adverb when it changes the claim, as *roughly* does for a reconstructed date. Where a stronger verb or a measured detail does the work, use it and cut the modifier. Keep a detail that changes what happens later. At Saltom the storm-damaged harbor explains why the miners sent coal back underground. End the scene once the consequence is clear.

Where a case permits more than one explanation, identify what each would lead us to expect, then return to a feature of the record that can help decide between them. Graeber and Wengrow introduce schismogenesis through the institutions of neighboring peoples and the historical possibility of deliberate differentiation. Take that argumentative move as a craft reference. Attribute a historical investigator’s thought experiment to its author. Work the comparisons needed to understand a claim into the chapter. Develop prospective study designs in the afterword and keep the manuscript’s own natural experiments in the research companion. [Wengrow on the comparison](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10192232/2/Wengrow_Historical_Materialism_DoE.pdf) · [Companion methods](../research/causal-inference.md) · [Evidence by chapter](../research/evidence.md)

Separate established observations, disputed interpretations, the book's causal hypothesis, and proposed tests. Energy, power, entropy production, useful work, reproduction, profit, and welfare require distinct accounting. State the system boundary, alternatives, and timescale. Do not invent dialogue, measurements, quotations, or empirical validation.

Preserve the distinction between conceptual contradiction and physical disequilibrium. Einstein's dialectical interpretation belongs to the book; relativity and wave–particle duality have distinct histories. State the proposed natural emergence of AI and data centers directly, then specify the mechanisms, prerequisites, and comparisons through which it can be tested. Their particular date, architecture, ownership, and uses require separate explanations. State that the universe and fundamental constants are probably assembled as the book's working conjecture; neither a numerical probability nor a derivation has been established.

Distinguish hierarchy, recurrence, recursive procedures, and self-modeling. A functional self-model does not by itself establish subjective experience. Trace the shift from explicit logical programs to learned representations without erasing the uses of formal methods. Keep learning-loss gradients separate from physical disequilibrium, and connect task improvements to actual physical consequences before drawing energetic conclusions.

Distinguish a model from its executing service, agent loop, and productive supports. Frozen model parameters do not imply fixed total inference compute. Separate prompting, reasoning-oriented training, and additional inference work; none guarantees unlimited cognitive improvement.

Give formation and further creation the most space. Specify what event the existing parts can produce, what it costs and how rapidly it occurs or is reversed. Trace what the outcome changes for the next event. Measure reproduction, durability and sustained dissipation separately. In a historical comparison identify who could pay for the first attempt, who received the return, and whether it could buy another attempt. Compare alternatives under the constraints known before the change.

Preserve the historical text of the [source notes](notes.md); append dated author clarifications when requested, as on November 17, 2026. Use the [expanded ideas](ideas.md) for research commitments, the [outline](outline.md) for the current sequence and the [source ledger](../research/sources.md) for evidence. Prior work on assembly, technology, biology, and social systems must be acknowledged.

## Review before committing

Read changed prose, check titles and transitions against the outline, verify local links and source keys, distinguish proposed designs from demonstrated effects, check identification assumptions and evidence limits, recalculate counts, and run `git diff --check`.

The manuscript remains readable without a runtime. The research code in [scripts](../scripts/README.md) uses a separate Python environment and has estimator/data-validation tests. Run `.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests -v` when changing that code. Passing software tests and reproducing a component comparison do not validate the universal hypothesis.
