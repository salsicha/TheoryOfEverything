# Illustration credits

## Newcomen engine

Newton Henry Black and Harvey Nathaniel Davis, *Practical Physics for Secondary Schools* (Macmillan, 1913), p. 219. The beam connects the piston on the right to a pump rod on the left. The illustration depicts an atmospheric engine of the Newcomen type. West’s later engine at Fowey Consols appears in the narrative. The 1913 plate is in the public domain in the United States. [Digitized illustration and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Newcomen6325.png)

## Jacquard portrait

Michel-Marie Carquillat after Claude Bonnefond, produced by Didier Petit et Cie, Lyon, 1839. Silk woven on a Jacquard loom. This edition uses a reduced-size reproduction of the whole portrait. Wikimedia Commons credits the photograph to Bonhams and marks the reproduction as public domain. [Image and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_la_m%C3%A9moire_de_J.M._Jacquard.jpg) · [Museum record for the design](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/219008)

## Lost City vent

Carbonate growth on a ledge at the Lost City hydrothermal field, December 2000. National Science Foundation, University of Washington and Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution. The public-domain image shows a formation sampled by the expedition. [Image and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lost_City_%28hydrothermal_field%2902.jpg) · [Expedition account](https://today.duke.edu/2000/12/alvind15.html)
